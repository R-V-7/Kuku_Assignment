1. Problem Choice, Framing & Strategic Boundaries

Why Rental Deposit Recovery? Over 70 million urban households in India rent their homes. Unlawful security deposit withholding is India's #1 urban consumer grievance. Landlords routinely withhold ₹20,000–₹1,00,000 using arbitrary pretexts ("painting", "cleaning", or "business losses"). While strong tenant protection exists under the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 and Indian Contract Act, 1872, hiring an advocate requires a ₹15,000–₹50,000 retainer—making private representation economically irrational for deposits under ₹1 Lakh. Tenants face immense intimidation and surrender their money.

Scope & Explicit Boundaries: In-Scope: Residential rental deposit disputes across all 28 Indian States & UTs (with auto-normalization for Delhi/Chandigarh). Covers PII redaction, conversational intake, dense query expansion, statutory vector retrieval, bounded evaluation, objective drafting (demand notice vs. settlement), WhatsApp pushback rebuttals, litigation ROI, and court dossier compilation. Out-of-Scope: Commercial property leases, active court evictions, and criminal fraud/forgery (which require police FIRs and forensic audit).

Sensible Assumptions Made: (1) Most urban tenants transfer deposits via UPI/NEFT, establishing admissible primary electronic evidence under Section 61 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA) even if written agreements are missing. (2) Domestic wear-and-tear is a landlord maintenance obligation under Section 11 MTA; deductions require genuine third-party contractor GST invoices. (3) Tenants routinely paste sensitive documents; client-side PII regex masking (Aadhaar, PAN, phone) is mandatory before LLM calls.

2. 5-Layer Micro-RAG Architecture & Division of Labor

To eliminate heavy infrastructure hurdles while guaranteeing zero hallucinations, we built a zero-external-database Micro-RAG pipeline running entirely in-memory with NumPy cosine similarity:

Pipeline Layer Handled by AI (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) Handled by Deterministic Code / Guardrails
Layer 1: Pre-Ingestion Guardrails & Fact Intake Extracts 9 structured fields from messy English/Hindi/Hinglish intake; dense statutory query expansion. Regex PII Redactor: Masks Aadhaar, PAN, phone, email before API calls. Pydantic tri-state logic (yes/no/unknown).
Layer 2: Human-in-the-Loop Clarification State Conversational understanding of natural language clarification replies. Clarification State Machine: Tracks missing fields, never asks twice. UT Resolver: Auto-maps Delhi/UTs; clears uncertainties.
Layer 3: Frictionless In-Memory Vector RAG Vector embeddings via text-embedding-004 (offline BM25 fallback). NumPy Vector Store: Cosine similarity search over verified statutes.json (ICA §73, MTA §11, BSA §61). Zero DB overhead.
Layer 4: Bounded Synthesis & Posture Routing Statutory summarization bounded strictly to retrieved <context> clauses; pushback roleplay. 3-State Machine: Classifies case as Unjustified (demand notice), Justified (discharge record), or Partial based on verified bills.
Layer 5: Post-Generation AST Citation Firewall Zero trust granted to raw generative model output. AST Citation Firewall: Scans citations via regex, checks whitelist, intercepts unverified statutes, logs latency/cost telemetry.

3. Key Product & AI Innovations (Beyond Generic Legal Chatbots)

1. Evidence Strength & Win-Probability Meter (evidence_meter.py)
Computes a dynamic 0–100% case recovery score under Indian evidence law: +25% for electronic bank trail (BSA §61), +20% for signed lease (ICA §10), and +10% for move-out date (TPA §108). If landlord produced valid contractor GST invoices, the score drops to 15% (Claim Unviable), actively warning against futile litigation.
2. Landlord Pushback Simulator & WhatsApp Rebuttals (simulator.py)
Solves the real-world question: "What do I do 10 minutes after sending the notice when the landlord pushes back?" Simulates the top 3 Indian landlord excuses (*"Court takes 10 years"*, *"Notice forfeiture"*, arbitrary repairs) and provides 1-click copyable 2-line WhatsApp rebuttals citing MTA §11, ICA §74, and DLSA Lok Adalat.
3. Litigation ROI & Forum Advisor (litigation_roi.py)
Prevents self-destructive litigation: warns against private lawyers for micro-claims (<₹15k), steering users to ₹0 DLSA Pre-Litigation Lok Adalat; recommends e-Daakhil Consumer Commission (₹0 court fee under ₹5L); calculates 8% p.a. statutory interest (Interest Act, 1978) for claims >₹60k.
4. One-Click e-Daakhil / DLSA Court Dossier (dossier.py)
When notices are ignored, 1-click compiles a complete, court-ready complaint petition (.md): Synopsis & Chronological List of Dates, Statutory Grounds (ICA §73, MTA §11, BSA §61), Prayer for Refund + Interest, Verification, and an interactive Index of Annexures checklist.

4. Tools Used & Real-World Failure Analysis (Mandatory Brief Requirement)

Tools Used: gemini-3.5-flash-lite, text-embedding-004, Claude, Google GenAI SDK, Pydantic, Streamlit, NumPy.

Where the AI Got It Wrong (Real Run Failure Analysis):
During testing of our Micro-RAG pipeline with gemini-3.5-flash-lite, the model committed a classic, dangerous legal AI hallucination: it escalated a routine civil rental contract dispute into a criminal offense, generating the following text:
"...withholding the security deposit constitutes unjust enrichment and criminal breach of trust under Section 405 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / BNS."
Even with strict prompt tags, Section 405 IPC was hallucinated from parametric memory (it did not exist in our retrieved statutes.json chunks). In Indian tenancy practice, framing a deposit refund dispute as a criminal offense is a fatal defect: civil courts and consumer commissions routinely reject criminalized rental petitions with strictures.

How Our Architecture Solved It: This real-world failure demonstrated that prompt engineering alone cannot guarantee legal safety. It necessitated our Layer 5 AST Citation Firewall:

  • Regex Statutory Backstop: Scans generated text for statutory and criminal patterns (Section \d+, Article \d+, IPC, BNS, criminal breach).
  • Deterministic Whitelist Enforcement: Matches detected citations against the exact retrieved chunk IDs in Layer 3. Because Section 405 IPC was not in the whitelist, the firewall physically intercepted the unverified criminal charge, replaced it with verified civil grounding under Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and logged the intervention in the telemetry dashboard.

Eliminating "Blind Advocacy" & "Concession Hallucinations": Generic legal AI blindly advocates for the user—generating aggressive legal demands even when a tenant admitted causing water damage and the landlord provided genuine contractor GST tax invoices. Conversely, loose keyword matching previously tricked models into conceding "utility bills" in cases where the landlord produced zero receipts. We solved this with a Strict 3-State Finite State Machine (draft.py & guidance.py):

  • 1. Unjustified (0% Proven): Arbitrary withholding (e.g. business losses, normal wear painting). Routes to a 15-Day Demand Notice for 100% refund with strictly zero concession language.
  • 2. Fully Justified (100% Proven): Landlord provided verified contractor GST invoices and meter bills. Routes to a Mutual Tenancy Discharge & Settlement Record; advises against wasteful litigation.
  • 3. Partially Justified (Mixed Evidence): Legitimate expenses are documented, but balance is arbitrary. Concedes only the verified invoices and demands the remaining unproven balance.

Conversational & Arithmetic Edge-Cases Solved in Testing:
(a) Substring Arithmetic Bug: Naive substring matching ('0' in reply) triggered on amounts containing zero (e.g. ₹15,000 or ₹40,000), resetting returned balances. Solved via regex boundary isolation (\b(0|zero|nil|none)\b) and auto-deriving max(0, deposit - deduction).
(b) Follow-up Field Overwrites: When users answered "no agreement" to follow-ups, earlier prompts misattributed the negative answer, overwriting state or city with "no". Solved via strict question-routing state machine guards.
(c) Bangalore "False Hope" Score: Our initial Evidence Meter gave a tenant 70% confidence simply because they had bank records and a signed lease, even though the landlord produced verified contractor GST invoices. We recalibrated the engine so that MTA §11 compliance gates the score to 15% (Unviable), actively protecting tenants from wasted litigation costs.

5. Honest Failure Modes & When Someone Needs a Real Lawyer

An honest AI lawyer knows its exact boundaries and tells the user when human legal representation is mandatory:

Failure Mode / Boundary System Limitation System Safeguard & User Advisory
Counterfeit / Forged Landlord Invoices The assistant cannot verify GST portal filings or perform forensic handwriting/receipt fraud analysis. Flags suspicious receipts; advises tenant to challenge authenticity via Section 65B affidavit before the District Consumer Commission.
Cash Deposits & Oral Leases When written_agreement == "no" and payment_proof == "no", electronic records under BSA §61 are absent. Informs user that civil claims require documentary custody proof; steers case to informal pre-litigation conciliation via DLSA Lok Adalat.
Active Evictions & Physical Intimidation Disputes involving lockouts, utility cut-offs, or criminal threats cannot be solved via a 15-day demand notice. Detects scope mismatch; advises tenant to file a police complaint and consult a human advocate immediately for an interim civil injunction.
NRI Landlords & Co-Living Aggregators In tech hubs (Bengaluru/Gurugram), landlords frequently reside overseas or operate through informal subletting startups. Physical Registered Post AD notices fail without domestic address; flags barrier and advises verified electronic notice (WhatsApp/email under BSA §61).
